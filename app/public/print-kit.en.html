A short guide for a live game of Mafia

Before the game

  1. Hand out the number cards — at random is fine. A number is only a player's label; in the app everyone has the same number as on their card. Speeches go in seating order around the table.
  2. The host does not play. Only the owner looks at a role; after the deal the phone goes back to the host.
  3. The host announces the roles in play, the first-night rules, whether eliminated roles are revealed, the alibi and the speech rules.

Night

Everyone closes their eyes. Roles wake up on the host's call and point at their target without words. The usual order: Mistress → Commissar → Doctor → Mafia with the Don → Don → Maniac. Whoever the Mistress visits makes no move that night. Only the roles in this game act.

Do not listen to the phone and do not peek. The host keeps the same pauses for every role, including eliminated ones when fake wake-ups are on.

Day

The host announces the results of the night. Then come last words, discussion in turn and nominations. Speak on your turn and respect the timer. Eliminated players do not help the living.

Voting

Show your vote on the host's call. Tie-breaks, abstaining and the alibi follow the game's settings. The host announces the result.

This guide does not replace the chosen rules. The exact role limits are in the app: "Host menu → Rules of this game".
